Our story

Loaded Goodies is a student‑led baking venture born from a deep love for creating sweet treats. Inspired by the skills passed down from a mother’s kitchen, our team has transformed that passion into a business that celebrates tradition and creativity. Every cookie we bake carries the warmth of family recipes and the excitement of young entrepreneurs eager to share their craft with the world.

Our mision

At Loaded Goodies, our mission is to share the joy of baking with our community by offering cookies that blend tradition with creativity. As a student‑led team, we are committed to turning the skills learned from family into a source of inspiration and opportunity. We strive to deliver treats that not only taste amazing but also bring people together, reminding everyone that the sweetest moments are baked with love.
